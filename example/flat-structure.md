# Flat Structure

#design #zettelkasten

Zettelkasten and wikis are conceptually flat. Hierarchies are antithetical to their purpose.

## The Problem with Hierarchies

Folder structures force artificial choices: "Programming" or "Learning"? "Python" or "Web Development"?

These decisions are arbitrary, rigid, and limiting. Most notes relate to multiple topics.

## The Flat Alternative

A flat directory with descriptive filenames, links, hashtag categories, and backlinks:

- **Allows multiple perspectives** - One note can have many categories
- **Encourages connections** - No artificial boundaries
- **Simplifies navigation** - Search and follow links, don't traverse trees
- **Matches thinking** - Ideas connect in networks, not hierarchies
