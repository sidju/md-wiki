# Note Taking

Effective note taking is essential for learning and knowledge management.

## Best Practices

- Write in your own words
- Use the [Zettelkasten Method](zettelkasten.md) for organization
- Review regularly
- Connect related ideas

## Tools

Various tools exist for note taking, from paper to digital solutions.
